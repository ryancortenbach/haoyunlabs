Lost package

If your tracking does not update for 7 days, email support@haoyunlabs.com with your order number. We open a USPS investigation and reship at our cost while the original package is in the carrier's hands.

Defective product or lost-in-transit shipments are covered by the lost-package process above and our quality guarantee. Email support with photos and we make it right.
