Privacy policy.

Effective May 21, 2026

This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and what control you have. Plain English, no buried clauses. If something is unclear, email support@haoyunlabs.com and we will answer.

1. Who we are

Hao Yun Labs is the operator of haoyunlabs.com and admin.haoyunlabs.com. Our mailing address is 414 Alder St, Suite 220, Portland, OR 97214. The fastest way to reach us about privacy is support@haoyunlabs.com.

2. What we collect

  • Name and email address you give us at checkout, signup, or in the affiliate program.
  • Phone number (optional, only if you provide it for shipping or support).
  • Shipping address you enter at checkout.
  • Order history (what you bought, when, for how much).
  • Payment card last four digits and card brand, returned to us by Stripe after a successful charge. We never see or store the full card number or CVC.
  • Affiliate referral code clicks (which code, when) so we can attribute commissions.
  • IP address and browser user-agent on form submits and account events, used only for anti-abuse and fraud detection.

3. How we use it

  • Fulfilling orders, printing shipping labels, and processing returns where applicable.
  • Sending order confirmations, shipping notifications, lot recall notices, and support replies.
  • Calculating and paying affiliate commissions.
  • Detecting fraud, chargeback abuse, account takeovers, and bot traffic.
  • Improving the site and customer experience based on aggregate behavior.

4. Who we share data with

We use a small list of named sub-processors. We do not sell personal data to third parties, ever, full stop.

  • Stripe for payment processing. Card data goes directly to Stripe and never touches our servers.
  • Shippo for printing USPS shipping labels and tracking events.
  • Resend for transactional and marketing email delivery.
  • Supabase for backend database and authentication.
  • Cloudflare for DNS, CDN, and DDoS protection.

We will update this list when it changes. If a sub-processor changes how it handles your data and the change affects you, we will note it here and on the effective date at the top of this page.

5. Cookies

We keep cookie usage minimal. The site uses an authentication session cookie when you sign in, and a 30-day affiliate-attribution cookie set when you visit with a ?ref= parameter so the original referrer is credited if you order. We do not run third-party advertising trackers or behavioral retargeting pixels on the main site.

6. Email preferences

Every marketing-style email we send includes a one-click List-Unsubscribe header that your email client can act on. You can also opt out at any time at haoyunlabs.com/unsubscribe. Transactional emails like order confirmations, shipping updates, password resets, and lot recall notices are tied to your account and cannot be turned off while the account is active.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, correct it if it is wrong, and ask us to delete it. These rights apply to all customers and include the rights granted by the California Consumer Privacy Act and the European Union General Data Protection Regulation where they apply to you. Email support@haoyunlabs.com with your request and we will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before we act on a deletion or access request.

8. Data retention

We retain order records for seven years to comply with United States federal and state tax requirements. Marketing contact data is deleted when you unsubscribe, with a 90 day suppression entry kept so we do not accidentally re-add you. Account login records, IP logs, and fraud-prevention signals are kept for 24 months and then aged out.

9. Children

Our products and site are not intended for anyone under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us data, email support@haoyunlabs.com and we will delete it.

10. Security

All traffic to and from our domains is encrypted in transit with TLS. Database storage at Supabase is encrypted at rest with AES. Card data is handled by Stripe under PCI DSS Level 1, and we do not store full card numbers or CVCs in our own systems. No security setup is bulletproof, but this is the baseline we operate to.

11. Changes

We will update this page when our practices change. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update. The URL haoyunlabs.com/privacy is the canonical location and will not move.

12. Contact

For any privacy question, data request, or concern, email support@haoyunlabs.com. We read every message and respond within a few business days.
